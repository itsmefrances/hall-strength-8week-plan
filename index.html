📈 WHAT COMES NEXT: Block 2 shifts into a power phase — plyometrics, jumps, and explosive work. The strength you build here is what Block 2 converts into rate of force development. That is the quality that wins the 1-minute record. Strength first. Power second. Record third.

The 8-Week Path

Weeks 1–2: Build patterns at load. Weeks 3–4: Climb harder. Week 5: Recover. Weeks 6–7: Serious phase. Week 8: Test your new personal bests.
