📍 The two intensity tests that matter most for AnC adaptation tracking

  • Wed Jun 10 (AnC #1): can you complete all 8×30s at 1:32–1:34 with the last 2 reps within 2s of the first? If yes, the system is loading well. If you crater at rep 5, we got the dose right but the adaptation hasn't landed yet — Week 4 dosing stays the same.
  • Sat Jun 20 (test): rep 1 result is the predictor. ≥321m says 325m is on the board for Weeks 5–6. 318–320m says we're tracking toward 322–323m and the timeline is honest. <318m says something's off — most likely nutrition adherence or sleep — and we look at the inputs before adjusting the plan.
